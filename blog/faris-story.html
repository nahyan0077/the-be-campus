From Plus Two to Dubai Accountant: How Faris Turned a 2-Year Job Search into a Gulf Career with Be Campus AI-ADIAT

Faris Kalathil
AI-ADIAT Graduate · Senior Accountant, Dubai · March 1, 2025 · 5 min read

My name is Faris Kalathil. I'm from Perinthalmanna in Malappuram district. I completed my BCom from a local college in 2022 and spent almost two years applying for accounting jobs — sending CVs, attending walk-ins, failing interviews. Nothing worked. I had a degree, but I had no practical skills that any employer actually wanted.

Today, I'm a Senior Accountant at a logistics company in Dubai, earning a package I never imagined possible from my hometown. This is the story of how Be Campus Academy and their AI-ADIAT program changed everything for me.

My Life Before Be Campus

After BCom, I thought getting an accounting job would be straightforward. I knew basic Tally. I understood the theory of accounts. What I didn't know was how completely disconnected my college education was from what employers actually needed.

The companies I interviewed at wanted GST filing experience. They wanted someone who'd used QuickBooks or Xero. Gulf companies wanted IFRS knowledge and experience with cloud accounting. I had none of it. I was competing against candidates who had done professional diploma courses and had practical experience. I kept losing.

By mid-2023, I was frustrated and honestly quite lost. A friend who had heard about Be Campus from someone in Malappuram town suggested I look into their AI-ADIAT course. I was sceptical — I'd seen plenty of "computer institutes" in Kottakkal that taught basic Tally and called it accounting training. But the AI-ADIAT was something completely different.

Why I Chose AI-ADIAT

When I visited Be Campus for a counselling session, the first thing that struck me was that the course content reflected what Gulf companies were actually asking for. QuickBooks. Xero. IFRS. UAE VAT and FTA compliance. International taxation fundamentals. And AI-powered accounting tools that big firms in the Gulf were already using.

The placement team was honest with me. They said: "If you want a job in Kerala, DPAT will get you there. If you want a Gulf accounting career, AI-ADIAT is what you need." They showed me placement records — real names and companies, not just vague claims. I enrolled the next week.

The Course: What I Actually Learned

AI-ADIAT is an 8-month program, and I want to be honest — it is intensive. The first two months cover advanced accounting and the transition from Indian GAAP to IFRS. This alone was something I had never encountered in BCom, and it immediately made me understand why my previous applications were failing.

Months three and four focused on QuickBooks Online and Xero — the two most widely used cloud accounting platforms in the Gulf, UK, and US. I learned everything from setting up a company file to generating financial reports, managing accounts receivable, and doing payroll. The trainers were practicing accountants, not just teachers — and the difference showed in every session.

UAE VAT was a dedicated module — understanding the FTA regulations, tax registration thresholds, filing GSTR-like returns under UAE law, and handling input tax credits. This module alone made me employable in the UAE in a way that no other course in Malappuram could have.

The AI module was genuinely fascinating. We learned to use AI-powered reconciliation tools that can match thousands of transactions in seconds. We used AI financial reporting dashboards. We learned how modern finance teams use automation to handle routine bookkeeping — and where human judgment remains irreplaceable. Understanding this distinction — what AI handles and what accountants still manage — is exactly what senior accounting roles require.

Course Highlight

The AI-ADIAT course at Be Campus is the only program in Kottakkal and Malappuram that combines IFRS training, QuickBooks, Xero, UAE VAT, and AI accounting tools in a single 8-month diploma. It is built specifically for students targeting Gulf and international accounting careers.

The Be Campus Difference

What set Be Campus apart was not just the content — it was the environment. The trainers were available for doubt-clearing beyond class hours. The placement team started working with me from month five onwards — updating my CV, coaching me on how to present my IFRS and cloud accounting skills in interviews, doing mock interviews with real questions from Gulf HR teams.

"The placement team at Be Campus knew exactly what Gulf companies were looking for, because they had placed students there before. They told me which interview questions to expect, how to talk about my QuickBooks experience, and even how to present myself for video interviews with Dubai-based HR managers."

— Faris Kalathil, AI-ADIAT Graduate

The Interview That Changed Everything

Three weeks after completing the AI-ADIAT program, I had my first interview with a logistics company in Dubai — done via video call. The HR manager asked me about UAE VAT returns, QuickBooks reporting, and IFRS 15 revenue recognition. I answered every question with confidence, because I had practiced all of these in class and had actual software experience to draw from.

I got the offer within 48 hours. My salary was four times what I had been offered in Malappuram-based accounting roles. Within three months of landing, I was promoted from Accountant to Senior Accountant based on my performance. The skills I learned at Be Campus made me one of the most technically capable people on the team from day one.

My Life in Dubai Now

I won't pretend everything has been easy. Moving to a new country is challenging. But professionally, I have never been more confident or more financially stable. I use QuickBooks and Xero every single day at work. I file UAE VAT returns for the company every quarter. The AI tools I learned at Be Campus — particularly the automated reconciliation and AI dashboard tools — have helped me streamline workflows that previously took my team hours.

My family's life has changed. My parents don't worry about finances anymore. My younger brother has enrolled in Be Campus's DPAT course, inspired by what happened to me. I visit Kottakkal when I can, and every time I do, I make it a point to speak with current Be Campus students.

My Advice to Students in Malappuram

If you're a BCom or commerce student sitting at home in Malappuram right now, unsure why your job applications keep failing — I know exactly how you feel. And I want to tell you: the gap isn't your intelligence. It's the skills gap between college education and what employers actually need.

Be Campus closes that gap. The AI-ADIAT isn't cheap. It isn't short. It requires real commitment over 8 months. But the return on that investment — for me — was a Gulf career that I couldn't have dreamed of two years earlier. Don't wait. Enquire, speak to the counsellors, and make the decision that changes your story.
